Before you walk in

Thirty years of stuff on the walls

Who's behind the bar

The greatest little bar in Pierce County

That's not our line — it's theirs, straight off their own page, signed “Captain Jack's Family.”

It's a family-run dive with a nineteenth-century-saloon streak: cedar posts, whiskey barrels turned into tables, tin signs stacked to the ceiling, a marlin over the bar and a mermaid on the sign. Locals call it eclectic. Everyone calls it comfortable.

A new owner took it over recently and has been putting money back into the place — customers noticed before anyone announced it. “The new owner has been working on the place and it really shows,” Brenda L. wrote on Yelp this August. Same staff people ask for by name, same $12 burger, better room.

Getting here

Come in, grab a barrel

  1. Just walk in

    No reservations, no dress code. Pull up a stool at a whiskey barrel or take the grass out back.

  2. Call ahead for a group

    Bringing eight or more, or want a table before a Monday trivia round? Ring the bar and they'll sort it.

  3. Ask what's on special

    Daily specials come off the kitchen, not the printed board — worth asking the second you sit down.
