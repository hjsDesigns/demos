The shop

On Main Street since '13

“We'll still have weekly new arrivals in store and if you are a customer from afar (hi you guys!) and see something you love posted on socials, please don't hesitate to give us a call and we'll be happy to ship to you!”

Bayabelle Boutique, in their own words

The white oval hanging off the Sumner town clock says it plainly enough — est '13. It has been the same corner of Main Street ever since.

Inside it's one big daylit room. Cream walls with a blush wash carried up over the ceiling, raw pine tables and benches, white-and-brass pendants, a navy floral rug and a whole wall of windows onto the street. Dresses and skirts down one side, knits and denim by the windows, and the small stuff — clips, earrings, candles, cards — laid out on the long table in the middle.

And nothing leaves in a plain bag. Every tag is printed on seed paper: “Plant this seed paper tag to grow wildflowers!”
