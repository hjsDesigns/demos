Meet the coaches

Black belt, Bonney Lake local.

John Proud has lived in Bonney Lake since 2008 and spent nearly two decades on the mats before opening Plateau Jiu-Jitsu in June 2024.

He earned his black belt from Jeff Hougland of Combat Sport & Fitness in December 2021, and opened this room with the support of his friend, mentor and coach. For John, jiu-jitsu is about the art and the longevity of the practice — a game you can keep getting better at for the rest of your life.

Shalise Ward, co-owner and coach, was awarded her purple belt by Jeff in January 2024. A gymnast from childhood, she competes throughout the year and teaches kids, adults and women — proof that technique, timing and consistent training can overcome size.
