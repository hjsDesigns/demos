    Why the chess pieces

    Jiu-jitsu is physical chess

    Continuously solving puzzles of positions and techniques. Three things every class comes back to.

    Movement

    Efficient movement first. The technique follows the hips.

    Breathing

    Slow down, breathe, see the whole board.

    Strategy

    Position before submission. One square at a time.
