The place

A hole in the wall — the good kind.

Thirty-four years on Mountain Hwy, and the regulars still call it their corner bar.

The Roundup sits on the Graham–Spanaway line with a maroon awning out front and wood paneling floor to ceiling inside. There's a kitchen behind the bar that turns out burgers, chowder and fish and chips, a couple of pool tables, darts, a jukebox, and a stage in the corner that gets used more than you'd think.

Kids are welcome at dinner. Monday bingo is family night, Thursdays every other week are kids' karaoke, and when the weather's right the fire pit out back is where everyone ends up. The t-shirts are cool too — the regulars say so.
