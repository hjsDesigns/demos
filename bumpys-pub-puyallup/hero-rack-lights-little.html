A small room that does a lot

Tiny bar. Long counter.

Google's own write-up runs one line and gets it right: a tiny, locally beloved dive bar with TVs, pub grub, burgers and specialty hot dogs. Here's what's actually going on in it.

Before you pull the door

Neon, plywood and pull tabs

It's written on their wall

The biggest little bar in Puyallup

That isn't our line. It's on their own sign above the pass-through and stitched across every hat on the shelf behind the bar.

One narrow room on E Main with the TVs going, a kitchen that turns out burgers, grinders and pizza all day, pull tabs on the counter and neon doing most of the lighting. Out back there's covered seating with heat, so the patio still works in November.

And framed on the wall like an award, right beside the flyers, is a five-star review they liked enough to print:

★★★★★

“Chef was rude, would eat here again”

Framed and hung behind the bar

Getting here

Downtown, on Main

  1. Just walk in

    No reservations, no list. It's a small room — early evening is when the counter has space.

  2. Come hungry before noon

    Breakfast comes off the same kitchen every morning and stops at twelve sharp.

  3. Ask what's on tonight

    Trivia nights and the specials aren't posted anywhere online. The bar knows.
