The shop

One barber. One chair.

Judy cuts hair on Cole Street, the way barbers did before anyone needed a booking app to get a trim.

There is one chair in here, which means one conversation at a time and one person responsible for how you walk out — which is exactly why the people who find her stop shopping around.
