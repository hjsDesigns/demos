Red sign, OPEN light

You order at the counter, under the screens. Everything below is what's on them.

What actually comes out

Village Fair Shopping Center

A counter, a dining room, and no pretending

White walls, wood trim, paper lanterns, a shoji screen behind the register and enough tables to sit a shift change.

It is a strip-mall teriyaki shop on South Meridian and it does not pretend otherwise. You order at the counter under the screens, you sit down or you take it with you, and the plate that comes out is the size people write reviews about.

The same family has been behind that counter a long time. Reviewers keep saying the same thing about it without being asked — “the same owners/family for years,” “I've been coming here for well over a decade,” “the lady behind the front counter always remembers us.”

Getting your food

Three ways, all simple

  1. Walk in and order at the counter

    The whole menu is on the screens above the register. Point at what you want.

  2. Call it in for pick-up

    Ring the shop, give them ten or fifteen minutes and it's boxed and waiting.

  3. Have it delivered

    The listing shows take-out and delivery both — ask which service is running that day.
