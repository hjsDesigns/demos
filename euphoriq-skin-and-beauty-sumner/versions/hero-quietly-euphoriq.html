Services

Skin first. Then the details.

Corrective skin work is the core of the studio — the kind that changes what you see in the mirror over months, not an afternoon. Lashes, brows and waxing finish it. Tap a line to see what's in it.

Facials

Hydrafacial, DMK enzyme therapy, dermaplaning and corrective facials built around your skin.
  • Hydrafacial™Cleanse, extract and hydrate in one pass under the ring light.
  • DMK Enzyme TherapyAdvanced skin revision — the long game for acne, scarring and texture.
  • Corrective facialsPersonalized to what your skin is doing right now.
  • DermaplaningPeach fuzz and dead skin off, product in.

Chemical peels

Professional-strength peels, planned as a series when scarring is the goal.
  • Professional chemical peelsStrength and frequency chosen for your skin, not a menu.
  • Peel seriesConsistent peels are how her clients work on old acne scarring.

Procell

Microchanneling for texture, tone and fine lines.
  • Procell microchannelingTiny channels, serum in, skin rebuilds itself. Usually done as a series.

Omnilux

LED light therapy — on its own or layered onto a facial.
  • Omnilux LEDRed and near-infrared light for calm, collagen and recovery.

Lash lift

Your own lashes, lifted and tinted — step by step, even if it's your first time.
  • Lash liftA curl from the root that lasts weeks, no extensions.
  • Lash tintDarker, fuller-looking lashes with nothing to put on in the morning.

Brows & waxing

Brow lamination, brow shaping and hair removal.
  • Brow laminationBrows set the way you'd brush them, for weeks.
  • Brow shapingTinted and shaped to the face in front of her.
  • WaxingFace and body hair removal.

Not sure which? Tell Katherine what you're seeing in the mirror and she'll pick the service — that's the first ten minutes of every visit.

Meet Katherine

She had the skin she treats.

A woman-owned studio in downtown Sumner, run by one licensed esthetician who got into this by fixing her own skin first.

“Like many others, I have struggled with my skin and worked hard to find solutions; this is what led to my decision to become an Esthetician. As I learned more about skin, my passion grew along with my knowledge.”

Katherine Matthews trained at the Euro Institute and opened Euphoriq as a one-room studio where every appointment is hers — the consult, the treatment, the aftercare talk. Her clients mention the same things: she explains what she's doing and why, she sends you home knowing what to use, and she knows skin that other places get wrong.

“As your Esthetician, I will always have your best interest in mind.”
