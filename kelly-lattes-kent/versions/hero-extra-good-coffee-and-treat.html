At the window

The faces you'll see more than family

Kelly said it in 2009 and it still runs the place: “You see your customers more regularly than your family.” Here's who's pouring.

Kelly & Chad McGraw

Started in Kent. Still first-name.

Kelly and Chad McGraw ran a small espresso stand in Kent, then bought two undeveloped acres along the Auburn–Black Diamond road near their house and built the second one from the dirt up. Black Diamond made three.

Kelly had already been pulling shots for twenty years when the Auburn stand opened in August 2009 — a covered, alpine-style patio with a fountain, a fire pit and river-rock benches, a front-service window, and a reader board facing the road. Chad runs his own paving company; the lot was the easy part.

Every cup still carries the mission statement and Kelly's own cell number. “People are shocked by that,” she said, “but I want to know how things are going with our customers.” Chad put it simpler: “We want to get to know our customers on a first-drink basis and first-name basis.”
