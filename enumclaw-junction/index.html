The crew

A little stand with a locomotive on it.

Enumclaw Junction has been the first stop of the morning for a lot of this town for a long time.

It's a drive-thru, so you never get out of the car — but the people in the window know your name, your size, and whether today is a try-something-new day. The energy drinks come in colors you can see from the road. The window gets hand-painted every month.

The stand's newest stop is a little farther up Rainier Ave, red sign and all — the same crew, the same board, a little more room to pull through.

    Specials

    The window changes every month.

    They paint it by hand. Stars, flags, a popsicle in July — and the drinks nobody else in town has a name for.

    Sour Blush in June. Butterfly Kisses in April, stirred or un-stirred. A wedding-cake latte when the leaves turn, eggnog cold foam when it's cold. The board above follows the calendar; the real one follows the crew.
