The truck

No dining room. Just a window that works.

Taqueria El Corral is a silver trailer with the name painted across it by hand, a blue OPEN sign in the glass.

The menu is painted, taped and marker-penned — a picture board for the plates, an orange board for the tacos, and a yellow sheet of especiales that changes when they feel like changing it. The meats run further than most kitchens around here bother with: cabeza and lengua sit on the same board as steak and chicken.

Reviews go back to 2019 and they still land the same way — people who moved away and drive back for it, and people who found it once in a parking lot and never went looking anywhere else.
