The shop

A small shop on East Main.

Jenna's Pet Grooming is a small grooming shop in downtown Auburn — not a chain counter, not a conveyor belt.

Dogs get washed, trimmed and handed straight back to the person who brought them. The reviews all say some version of the same thing — thoughtful, careful, goes the extra mile — and one of them says she'll carry your dog out to your car if you can't come in and get it.

In July 2026 the shop was nominated for Best Pet Grooming in the CommunityVotes Auburn awards, and Jenna posted a thank-you to everyone who voted for her.
