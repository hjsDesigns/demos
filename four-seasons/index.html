Order or come in

Call it in, or take a booth

  1. Phone the kitchen

    They'll tell you how long it'll be. Take-out adds $1.50 to most soups and noodle sides.

  2. Pick a dinner or go à la carte

    The family-style dinners are minimum two of the same. Everything else comes with rice.

  3. Card or cash

    Visa, Mastercard and Discover. No checks — it's printed right on their menu.
