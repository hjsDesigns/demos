    The lanterns, and what comes out

    The last one standing

    Five sister restaurants. One left.

    A regular counted them off in his own review — Dynasty's in Federal Way, Ming Tree in Bellevue, Forbidden City in Puyallup, Lotus Tree in Auburn, The Great Wall in Tacoma. And Four Seasons.

    Rick S. wrote it down on Yelp in February 2025: “All are gone now except 4 seasons. I go every opportunity!!” The same old-school Chinese-American cooking, the same green booths, the same lanterns over the same tables.

    Bing is the one who answers questions on their Yelp page — signed “Bing P., Business Owner.” Ming runs the place alongside him, and regulars thank Helen and Sara by name. There's a second name on their own Facebook page, too: The Four Seasons Restaurant and Maui Room.
