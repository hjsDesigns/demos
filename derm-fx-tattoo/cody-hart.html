Cody Hart · owner & tattooer

A life in
the craft.

Auburn, through and through

Drawing became
a way of life.

Cody grew up around tattoo shops with his father. The interest became an apprenticeship, and the two later opened a shop together.

His approach is personal: understand why a piece matters to the person wearing it, then give that idea his attention.

Derm F/X has been at its Auburn location since 2005. Cody’s studio includes work in color, black and grey, and custom designs.
