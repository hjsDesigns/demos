The work

Black and grey, full color, lettering, cover-ups, fine line. Nothing off a flash sheet unless you want it that way — the sign on the door says all custom work and they mean it.

Who's holding the machine

Cody Hart runs the shop

Auburn kid, Auburn shop. It started as a father-and-son thing, and it has never moved off this block.

Cody is the owner, and the pieces he finishes get signed — you'll see “By Cody Hart” in the corner of his photos, which is how the work on this page could be credited to him honestly instead of guessed at.

The shop's own line about the place, written by them: “Our goal is to provide our customers with a comfortable and safe environment, quality artwork, and amazing customer service.” They put money back into the town too — their statement mentions charitable fundraisers, “because we live here as well.”

He isn't the only one on the bench. Reviews left this summer ask for Sarah, Dylan and Felipe by name, and the piercings are done by Chris Cricket. Ask at the counter who's free — or who's right for what you're after.

Before you come in

How it works here

No app, no online calendar. Their own written policy: appointments are made in person, at the shop.

  1. Walk in and talk it through

    Bring the idea, a reference, a rough size and where you want it. You book the sitting at the counter, face to face with whoever's going to do it.

  2. Leave a deposit

    Their statement puts the minimum at $50 to hold the appointment. It comes off the price of the piece.

  3. Stencil, then needle

    The drawing gets sized and laid on your skin first, so you see exactly where it sits before anything is permanent.
