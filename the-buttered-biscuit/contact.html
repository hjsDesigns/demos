Before you visit

A few things
to know.

Where can I see the menu?

Browse breakfast, lunch, kids’ favorites, gluten-free selections and drinks on our menu page. The complete catalog and current ordering options are on Toast.

Are there gluten-free choices?

Yes, the restaurant lists a separate gluten-free menu. The kitchen also handles gluten, so cross-contact is possible. Speak with your server about your dietary needs and available options.

Is there a kids’ menu?

Yes. Kids’ choices include pancakes, French toast sticks, chicken tenders, grilled cheese and more. See the kids’ choices →

How do I check hours or plan for a group?

Call (253) 826-6099 for today’s hours and to ask about your group. Arrangements and availability are confirmed directly by the restaurant.
