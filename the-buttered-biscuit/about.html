Our story

A place with people at its heart.

The meal brings you in. The familiar faces make it feel like your place.

In the words of Tami Haskins

“This is like people’s place to come and feel paid attention to and special.”

Tami Haskins, identified as owner in FOX 13’s July 2019 report.

That visit offered a glimpse beyond the big breakfasts. Haskins pointed out photographs of longtime regulars and spoke about the people who made the restaurant part of their everyday lives.

One framed photograph had been brought in by the son of two customers who had passed away. Her parents also had a place on the menu. In that story, the dining room becomes a kind of family album.
