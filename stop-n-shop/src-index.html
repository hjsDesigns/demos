The blue farmhouse

A farmhouse doing a country store's work.

No chain, no franchise — a turn-of-the-century farmhouse at a rural crossroads, painted powder blue with red diamonds on the trim, pumps where the front yard would be.

It's family-run, and the family is actually here — behind the counter, morning to close, the kind of owners regulars mention by habit. First light to 10 PM, they're the stop for the farms, the fishermen, the commuters, and everyone who'd rather not drive into town.
