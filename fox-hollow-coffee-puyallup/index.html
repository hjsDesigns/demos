What comes out the window

Cups people drove out of their way for

The stand

One small building, a green roof, a red OPEN sign

It sits on the corner of 112th and Woodland, and it has been doing one thing for a long time: handing coffee out a window.

The fascia along the front spells it out in six words — latte, mocha, americano, coffee, granita, espresso. Under it, the wall is a big black-and-white mural of an old cafe scene, and the FH oval hangs off the corner where you can read it from the road. The window says OPEN DAILY, and it means it.

The coffee in the cup is Fox Hollow Coffee, a Washington roaster whose oval is printed on every lid, sleeve and cup that comes out the window. The stand itself trades as Woodland Espresso, L.L.C., out of Puyallup.

Two hundred and twenty-one people have rated it on Google. It averages 4.6.
