No appointment

Wash your own

Walk in and take an open tub. They're waist-high and wood-clad, so nobody's kneeling on a bathroom floor. Shampoo and conditioner are supplied, aprons are provided, and there are tables for brushing and blow drying at the far end of the room.

  • First come, first serve — no booking
  • Shampoo & conditioner supplied
  • Aprons provided
  • Brushing and blow-dry tables
See the seven steps

By appointment

We'll do it

Full service grooming runs by appointment only. Call first and they'll talk the dog through with you — their words: “it's important to chat with you about your dog to make sure we are a good fit and provide a positive experience.”

  • Full service grooming, appointment only
  • Nail trims on a walk-in, while groomers are available
  • Big dogs and small dogs both
