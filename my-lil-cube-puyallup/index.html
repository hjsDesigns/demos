The room

Wood tables, big windows, Meridian out front

It's a little room. That's the whole idea — it's called My Lil' Cube.

Slate walls, honey-wood tables, black chairs, wood blinds, and a couple of pendant lamps over the counter. RAMEN and RICE are spelled out in metal letters on the back wall, with a Hokusai wave and a Sapporo poster beside them. From the tables in front you're looking straight out at N Meridian.

It sits a block up from downtown Puyallup and a short walk from the fairgrounds, with a Pierce Transit stop at the pole sign out front. Four and a half stars would be a good restaurant. They're carrying a 4.7 across more than sixteen hundred Google reviews — for a room this size, that's a lot of people going out of their way.
