Meet Michelle

Fourteen years, mostly by referral

Michelle Walk opened GLO in Maple Valley in 2011 and built it one referred client at a time.

Her words: a boutique-style holistic destination — hair removal, skin care and body work, with an unwavering dedication to clean and organic products. No walk-ins, so the room is yours for the whole appointment.

She's thorough in the old-fashioned way. One client's review: she flagged a questionable mole during a wax and sent her to a dermatologist. That's the clinic in one sentence.
