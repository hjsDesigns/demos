Before your appointment

Good care starts
with a conversation.

GLO books by appointment, with no walk-ins. Here’s a useful place to begin when you get in touch.

01 · Your service
Tell Michelle whether you’re interested in a facial, a skin treatment, waxing or sugaring. If you’re unsure, ask about a consultation.
02 · Your questions
Ask about the current price, what the appointment includes and any preparation. Facial prices are discussed at consultation; the saved hair-removal menu is a starting point to confirm.
03 · Your time
Share a few days that suit you. Evening events are on announced dates, so check directly with GLO before planning an evening visit.
