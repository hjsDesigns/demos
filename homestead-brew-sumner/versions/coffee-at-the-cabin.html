The stand, just as you’ll find it

Cedar walls.
A familiar window.

A small stand with a place in your everyday.

Look for the cedar siding, the metal roof and the Homestead sign. Drive up for a cup to go, or stop at the walk-up window.

Stick with a latte or mocha, or make it a White Zombie — the cabin’s white chocolate and white coffee signature. Chai, hot chocolate and Italian sodas give the non-coffee drinkers something to choose, too.

A few cabin essentials

First time here?

Something besides espresso?

Chai, hot chocolate, apple cider, tea and steamers are on the board. Italian sodas bring a cold, colorful option for the road.

Can I make this a food stop, too?

Yes — the menu includes breakfast burritos, breakfast and bagel sandwiches, hot dogs, polish dogs and pastries. Ask what’s available when you arrive.

What’s the White Zombie?

One of the cabin’s house drinks: white chocolate and white coffee. The Chocolate Charger pairs chocolate with white coffee instead.

Where are the current prices?

At the window. The online board shows the drink and food selection; the team can confirm current prices and specials.
