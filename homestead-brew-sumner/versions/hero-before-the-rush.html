The stand

The little cabin in the McLendon lot.

A cedar hut with a metal roof, two windows, and a line of trucks before the hardware store even unlocks its doors.

Homestead Brew pours Dillanos — roasted a few blocks away in Sumner — and builds the rest on top of it: lattes, mochas, white coffee signatures like the White Zombie, Italian sodas for the kids in the back seat, and a weekly special on the board. Hot dogs and polish dogs with the whole condiment list, breakfast burritos and bagel sandwiches for the early crowd.

The reviews keep saying the same two things: the coffee comes out the same every time, and the people at the window remember you. That's the whole idea of a homestead.
