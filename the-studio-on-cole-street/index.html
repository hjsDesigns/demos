Whose room this is

Madison keeps it ready

Madison Hall runs The Studio on Cole Street — one room in downtown Enumclaw that anyone with a camera can have for an hour or a day.

She is a photographer herself, so the room is set up the way a photographer would want it: the light left where it works, the sofa and the backdrop already standing, a mirror by the door for the last check before a family walks in front of the lens.

Most weeks it is other people's work coming out of here — newborn sessions, maternity, holiday and spring minis, the odd local business needing product shots. The studio stays quiet, clean and booked, and Madison stays the person who answers.
