Meet Lulú

She taught herself to bake, then opened the door.

Lourdes “Lulú” Hernández ran a cottage bakery out of her own kitchen before she moved it into a storefront on South 96th Street.

She is self-taught. The shop opened roughly two years before South Sound Magazine came to visit in 2024, with help from the small-business programs at the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce — she is a member there to this day.

Her daughter Camila works the counter beside her — a mother-and-daughter shop, not staffed help. When a customer left a note about Camila on their page, it was Lulú herself who wrote back.
