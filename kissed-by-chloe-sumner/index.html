The shop

On Main Street since 2016

“Brick & Mortar boutique in the heart of Sumner where Boho & Western collide.” — their own line in the Sumner Main Street Association directory.

It's a western shop with the edges knocked off — boots and hats and denim, but hung off iron gates and painted dressers instead of chrome rails, with a splash of prairie in the dresses. The furniture holding the clothes is usually for sale too.

Look up when you get there. The name over the door is spelled out in salvaged marquee letters bolted to a strip of old corrugated tin — a cream K, a red i, a galvanised S, a big red S — and the shop name is hand-painted across the front window in white brush script.
