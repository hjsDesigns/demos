# Milton Lodge — the room, the plate, the next visit

The actual Lodge supplies the design: dark timber and charcoal, blue bar seating, amber beer and a cream menu sheet. Barlow Condensed gives the headings the weight of a neighborhood bar sign; DM Sans keeps the useful details clear. This remains the factory template, with its shared header, navigation, sections and responsive foundation.

The journey starts inside the room on desktop and with the actual burger plate on phones. The arrival band answers where, when the day starts and how to confirm closing. Plated food leads directly into a paper menu, whose three buttons reveal verified dishes. The blue room spread then introduces the bar before the documented 2018 history and short, sourced review excerpts. A final patio photograph supports the obvious phone and directions actions. Each change of material has a job in that progression.
