The Lodge story

A familiar corner.
A fresh start.

Shawna Bell opened the Milton Lodge in the building that once held the Milton Tavern. A renewed room, breakfast, lunch and dinner gave the corner its next chapter.

The room tells the rest: blue half-walls, wood tables and people gathered at the bar.
