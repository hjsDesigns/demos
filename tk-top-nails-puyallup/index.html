Inside

A wall of colour by the door

It's a small shop, and it's set up the way a small shop should be.

Booking

Walk in, or call ahead

  1. Just walk in

    The window says Walk-Ins Welcome and so does the sign at the driveway. No appointment needed for a manicure or a spa pedicure.

  2. Call if there's a few of you

    Two or three of you together, or coming in close to closing? Ring first and they'll have the chairs ready.

  3. Ask what today's price is

    Nail prices aren't posted anywhere online. Ask when you call — they'll tell you before you sit down.
