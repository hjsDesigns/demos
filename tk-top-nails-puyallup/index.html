Inside

A wall of colour by the door

It's a small shop, and it's set up the way a small shop should be.

Spa pedicure chairs down one wall under warm pendant lamps, manicure stations down the other, a couch by the window and the TV on. Cream walls, tile floor, plants in the corner.

The rack by the door is the whole catalogue — the Polish Center, hundreds of bottles deep, with a second rack of dipping powders beside it. Point at the one you want.
