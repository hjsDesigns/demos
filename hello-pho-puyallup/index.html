The room

Walnut, cream and a lot of space.

Hello Pho is women-owned and Asian-owned, and it is a big, warm room.

A slatted walnut bar sits in the middle under a black ceiling edged in copper, with woven pendant lights, booths along one wall and plenty of tables.

Stay for a bowl, take it curbside, or order online for delivery. Hello Pho is also LGBTQ+ friendly, according to its Google profile.
