The Harts

The second Carl Hart

Most accounting firms don't get tested the way this one did.

Carl Hart Sr. built the practice from nothing — a Western Washington accounting degree, ten years learning the work under other people, then his own shingle in a farm town that already had somewhere to get taxes done. What he had instead was that people liked handing him the shoebox. Twenty years later, half of Enumclaw's returns went through that office.

He died in August 2019, and the practice didn't. His wife and son were already in the building — and seven years on, the same family is still filing the same clients' returns under the same name. That's the whole pitch, really: whoever did your taxes in 2010 is, near enough, still the one doing them now.
