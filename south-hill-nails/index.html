Who's in the chairs

Vincent, Ruby and Linda

Three names come back again and again in the reviews — and the people writing them have been sitting in these chairs a long time.

Parks counts ten years: “still the only place i will go.” Carol Goodine counts over eight, and calls it “not just a nail salon; it's a place where you're treated like family.”

It's a walk-in shop in the Meridian Square strip — one landline, one door, red letters and a yellow oval over a window lettered in gold. Bring a photo of the set you want; regulars say they're “masters at recreating whatever you ask for.”
