The shop

You'll know it by the cedar arbor

Red letters, a yellow oval and a window lettered in gold, tucked into the strip on the east side of the highway.

It's a walk-in shop with a landline and no app. Everything — the color you want, whether there's a chair free, what a full set runs today — goes through one phone call.

There's no website, no online booking and no price list anywhere on the internet. Everything anyone knows about this place, they learned by walking through the door.

Getting in

    Tell them what you're after and roughly when you want to come in.

  2. Or just walk in

    Walk-ins are taken as chairs open up — earlier in the day is quieter.

  3. Bring a picture

    A photo of the set you want saves everyone ten minutes at the chair.
