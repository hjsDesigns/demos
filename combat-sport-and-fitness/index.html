    Our story

    Every stripe earned.

    We have been training men, women and children in this community and outlying areas since we established our business in 2008.

    Coaching staff and volunteers are highly trained and vetted, and we pride ourselves on the skill set and well-oiled machine we've been building all these years. Ask anyone in the business of BJJ, MMA and those who understand the art — if you want to be the best, train with them.

    We understand there are other choices out there, and we truly appreciate your time and business. As true practitioners of the sport, we don't feel the need to "give" out trophies. Every stripe, belt and win is earned and never bought. You can be sure that when you receive one here, you can go out and compete at the level you are and feel good about it.

    We build confidence and a sense of family here. If that feels like a good fit for you, come on by.

    — Jeff Hougland, owner · UFC vet · black belt
