Classes

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu for kids and adults, Fighter Fit conditioning, invite-only MMA, private lessons and yoga through a partner studio.

Ages roughly 5 and up

Discipline, confidence, and a belt that means something — three afternoons a week.

Jiu-jitsu is a ground-based martial art. Kids learn to use leverage, angles, pressure and timing — and a working knowledge of how the body moves — to control a bigger, stronger partner and finish a position without anyone getting hurt. It's excellent self defense, and it builds the kind of confidence that shows up at school, not just on the mat.

We don't hand out promotions here. Every stripe and every belt is earned, so when your kid receives one they can go compete at that level and feel good about it.

  • WhenMonday, Wednesday & Friday at 5:00 pm · 55 minutes
  • WhoBoys and girls, roughly 5 and up, no experience needed
  • BringA gi (ask the coach before buying one) and water
  • Price$130 a month, 3 days a week — see pricing

Gi & No-Gi

Gi three nights a week, no-gi on Thursdays, open mat on Saturdays. All levels, men and women.

A ground-based martial art built on leverage, angles, pressure and timing, and on knowing how the human body works — so you can submit an opponent without violence. It's real self defense for men and women, and it's the same curriculum our competitors come up through.

Invite only

Pro and amateur fight training. You don't sign up for this one — you earn your way in.

Mixed Martial Arts training for pro and amateur fighters, by invitation only. If you're ready to compete and a coach thinks you're ready, they'll bring you in.
