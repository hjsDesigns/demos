Under the sign

A bar you can spend the night in

Tables to play on, a yard to sit in, and a kitchen that stays on while you do.

Pool, darts & cornhole

Pool tables and dart boards inside, cornhole boards out back. Regulars say the tables are kept up.

Karaoke Friday & Saturday

It's painted on the banner strung over the stage: every Friday & Saturday night. The board below tells you if it's tonight.

The beer garden

A big fenced yard behind the building — picnic tables, umbrellas up, food carried straight out to you.

Trivia nights

Trivia runs here too, and every screen in the place is on for the game — their sign out on Main has read FOOTBALL HERE all season.

The board out on Main

Is it karaoke tonight?

Wayne's still sets its sign by hand, one plastic letter at a time. Tap the board to switch the lamps on.
