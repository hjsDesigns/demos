Under the sign

A bar you can spend the night in

Tables to play on, a yard to sit in, and a kitchen that stays on while you do. Tap a heading and the rest of it rolls out.

Five things, whenever you're in

Regulars say the tables are kept up — Kammi Oderin's words on Google: “Darts and pool are always kept up.”

  • Pool tables
  • Dart boards
  • Cornhole boards out back
  • The claw machine — “a personal fav” (Mel G)
  • Room to dance when there's a DJ on

Karaoke Friday & Saturday

Off the banner over the stage

It's painted up there in two lines: every Friday & Saturday night. The board further down tells you whether it's tonight.

Out back, year round

A fenced yard behind the building, and it stays usable when the weather turns.

  • Long picnic tables, some of them covered
  • Umbrellas up through the summer
  • Heaters going on cold nights (Mel G)
  • A TV out there for the game (Jaquan Satchel)
  • Food carried straight out to you

Trivia & the game

The board out on Main

Is it karaoke tonight?

Wayne's still sets its sign by hand, one plastic letter at a time. Tap the board to switch the lamps on.

Karaoke every Friday & Saturday night — off the banner strung over the stage. Any other night, the board points at the next one.
