What she does

Grooming, start to finish.

“Pet grooming is my passion. I've been grooming Cats and Dogs and basically all pets since 2006, I love all animals…” — Robin, in her own words. Tap a line to open it.

The poodle with the red bow, the wheaten on the bed, Mr. Darcy in his bandana — every dog on this page came off Robin's table. Bath, clip, ears, nails, bandana on the way out.

Cats get groomed here too, not just dogs — Robin has been doing both since the day she started, along with whatever else people bring her.

That pink-and-purple tail at the top of the page is hers. If you want a little color on your dog, ask her about it when you call.

Robin's own rule: “Contact me to schedule a consultation prior to making your first appointment.” She meets your pet first, then books the groom — so nobody's surprised on the day.

Every coat is different, so pricing gets settled at the consultation — ask her when you call.

Meet the groomer

Twenty years of somebody's whole family.

“Pet grooming is my passion.”

Robin has been grooming since 2006 — cats, dogs, and basically all pets. Not a chain, not a rotation of staff: one groomer who does the whole appointment herself, in a small groom shop in her own home here in Kent.

That's why the first step is a consultation instead of a booking. She'd rather meet your pet, see the coat, and hear what you want before anything gets scheduled.
