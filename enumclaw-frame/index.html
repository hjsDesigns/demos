The part nobody explains

What actually happens after you get hit

Three steps. That's the whole thing.

  1. Bring it by, or call first

    Say what happened and what the car is doing. The shop looks at it and writes it up — you don't need to know what's broken before you get here.

  2. The claim goes through the shop

    Enumclaw Frame works with insurance claims, so the paperwork and the adjuster go through here instead of through you.

  3. Structure, then panels, then paint

    Frame work comes first. The car gets measured and pulled back to square before any panel or paint work goes over the top of it — because paint over a bent car is just a nicer looking bent car.

One thing worth knowing

You choose the shop. Not the insurance company.

Your insurer can recommend a repair facility. In Washington, the decision about who repairs your vehicle is yours.
