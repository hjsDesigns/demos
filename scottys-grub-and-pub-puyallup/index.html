Who's in here

Old school meets new school

A downtown dive that stopped redecorating on purpose — and everybody who walks in is glad it did.

Scotty's has been on this block a long time. The oval sign bolted to the brick outside says Serving Puyallup since and then a year the plants have grown over — but the building is 1890s downtown brick, and the room has thirty years of somebody-left-it-here stacked on every wall.

It's the kind of room where the bartender is the reason people come back. Read the reviews and they don't say “the bartender” — they say Rachel, Lexi, Korie, Mel, by name. There's no air conditioning, and nobody seems to mind.

Once a month the Cruise Puyallup car show runs right down Meridian past the front door, and the sidewalk chairs out front turn into the best seats on the street.

Getting here

North Meridian, middle of downtown

  1. Just walk in

    No reservations. Street parking runs the length of Meridian and there's plenty of it.

  2. Ring ahead for a group

    Bringing eight or more, or want the back room around the pool tables? Call the bar and ask.

  3. Ask what the kitchen's got

    The full food list isn't posted anywhere online — ask at the bar and they'll tell you what's on tonight.
