Our story

Two shops. One family.

Tacoma Java Co. is a family-owned coffee company. Bonney Lake is its second shop, and the one with a room to stay in.

How it started

From 6th Ave to Bonney Lake.

Leilani and Anthony Hall own Tacoma Java Co., and the family has run it since 2016.

The Tacoma shop had to find a new home in 2022, and the family rebuilt on 6th Avenue. Then they opened a second shop on Veterans Memorial Drive in Bonney Lake: a sit-down café with a library corner behind a glass garage door.

“To all of our new neighbors in the Bonney Lake community, thank you…”Tacoma Java Co., on Facebook
