Inside and out back

Two rooms, one pub

Pull in under that sign and there are two of them. Inside is the dark bar with the TVs on. Out the back it opens up into a covered beer garden big enough for pool tables, a stage and a long row of picnic tables — same kitchen feeding both.

The Cabin

What happens at the Cabin, stays at the Cabin

That's not our line. It hangs on their wall, and it's the whole bio on their own Linktree.

The pub sits on E Valley Highway between Sumner and Auburn, with a lot big enough to find a spot in. Up front it's dark plank walls and a long bar, televisions running whatever's on, neon down the wall and pool tables going most nights.

Out the back it opens up. A covered beer garden runs the length of the building — bar stools and picnic tables, patio heaters for the cold months, painted murals down the back wall, and a stage at the end for when a band is in. Regulars will tell you the rest.
