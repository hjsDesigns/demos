Inside 1325 Cole

Not a window. A room.

Glossy deep-red tile the whole length of one wall, a black stone counter with the syrup shelf above it, and tables that are yours for as long as you want them. Most coffee in this valley is handed through a window — this one comes with a chair.

Behind the counter: whole beans on the steel shelf, a wall of syrups, a pastry case, and a cooler for whatever you're grabbing on the way out.

“Locally sourced foods — Sunrise Bites, Bonnie's Buns, Red Tea Room, & house made sauces!”
