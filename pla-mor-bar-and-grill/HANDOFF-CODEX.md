### PROVISIONAL / corrections to what was briefed

1. **Sunday is 11 AM – 10 PM, not 11 AM – 2 AM.** Restaurantji, Apple Maps and Waze all agree on
   10 PM; the "Sunday 2 AM" line in the old hit list is superseded. Shipped as 10 PM.
2. **The DePasquale story is not on the page.** Fred DePasquale, the $24,000 purchase in 1966 and the
   family's ownership could not be corroborated by any source in hand — the draft's own research
   reached the same conclusion. Only "SINCE 1966", which is printed on their menu, ships.
3. **The KING 5 connection is real and photographed** — the "Best of Western Washington · BEST DIVE
   BAR · PLA-MOR BAR AND GRILL · voted best of western washington in the annual viewers poll ·
   K5 Evening" banner is on their wall in g1. No year and no repeat-win claim is made.
4. **Rating shown is 4.5 across 250 (Restaurantji)** — that's the figure verifiable in the sources in
   hand. Google reportedly shows 4.4; swap it if Hayden prefers the Google number.
5. **The tank is real; the piranha claim is not settled.** g43 shows an armoured catfish on driftwood,
   g60 shows the painted fish wall. The piranha on the page is the one printed on their own menu. The
   copy says exactly that and claims nothing about the tank's contents.
6. **Specials prices withheld** (see above) — the sheet is older than the menu.
7. Photo rights: every image is an uncleared asset off their Google listing. Permission before launch.
