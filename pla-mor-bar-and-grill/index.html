Before you walk in

Dark wood, and a fish tank

The date on the menu

Since 1966

It's printed right under the name on the front of their own menu: SINCE 1966.

You can read the rest of it off the bar. The top is set under glass and packed with old wooden drink tokens from taverns all over the plateau — Ski-Inn, Enumclaw. Enumclaw Saloon, 825-2331. Hager's Tavern, 418 N.W. 65th St. Maple Valley Tavern. Sno-Valley Eagles #3529. The Yardarm Pub & Broiler, out in Des Moines. Most of those rooms are gone. This one still opens at eleven.

And there's a piranha on the front of the menu — the same fish that turns up on the wall and in the tank in the corner. Nobody explains it. Nobody has to.
