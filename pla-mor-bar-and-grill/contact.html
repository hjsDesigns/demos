Before you come by

Can I order food to go?

Takeout is available. Call the bar to confirm your order and pickup timing. The printed menu lists a $1.25 charge per to-go order.

How late is the kitchen open?

Bar hours and kitchen hours can differ. Call to check food service for the time you plan to arrive.

What’s on tonight?

See the official Facebook page for recent posts, or call to ask what’s happening.

Bringing a group?

Call ahead about your group size and timing. Online reservations aren’t offered here.
