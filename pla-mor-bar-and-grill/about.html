The Pla-Mor story

A MAPLE VALLEY
FIXTURE.

Pla-Mor’s own menu carries the words “Since 1966.” The room carries the rest: a wood bar, a well-known aquarium, and the easy feeling of a place that belongs to its town.

The menu runs from burgers and Reubens to salads and a ribeye dinner. Inside, you can settle at the bar or gather around a table. It’s a simple invitation to spend a little more time together.
