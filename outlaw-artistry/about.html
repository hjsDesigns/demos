The person behind the door

Hi, I’m Brooke.

A comfortable space. Personal attention. Care that feels like you.

Brooke created Outlaw Artistry to be a place where you can relax, feel comfortable and leave feeling your best.

She has worked in esthetics since 2017 and continues to learn, refine her skills and stay current with techniques and treatments. Her specialties include customized facials, advanced skin treatments, waxing, brow laminations and lash lifts.

One studio. One esthetician. Your appointment is with the owner herself.
