Before you call

Read your sidewall.

Every tire has its size molded on the side — something like 265/70R17. Slide the numbers to match yours and we'll show you what they mean. Then call us with it and we can quote you on the spot.

Your size

265/70R17

Common around here

31.6"Overall height
7.3"Sidewall
10.4"Tread width
  • 265 — section width in millimeters, sidewall to sidewall.
  • 70 — aspect ratio: the sidewall is 70% as tall as the tire is wide. Lower number, shorter sidewall.
  • R17 — radial construction on a 17-inch wheel.

The penny test.

Put a penny in the groove, Lincoln's head down. If you can see the top of his head, you're at 2/32" — the legal minimum — and it's time. Slide to see where yours is.

The shop

Why give your business to a chain?

Enumclaw Tire has been a three-man operation since 1984. No service writers, no production line, no high-school kids on your lug nuts — the guy who quotes you is the guy who does the work. Les Schwab is a block up the street; people drive past it to get here.

Tim runs the alignment rack — classics, lifted trucks, anything with nice wheels you don't want torn up. Paul and Greg handle the tires and the counter, and they'll tell you straight when something can wait another season.
