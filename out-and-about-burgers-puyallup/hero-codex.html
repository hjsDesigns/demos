What you're pulling into

A wagon, a window, and a line of cars

No dining room, no patio, no counter. Everything happens at one window on the side of a white catering trailer parked off Meridian East.

Drive thru only

Their sign puts it in four words: Drive Thru Only Please. Walk-up service isn't provided — stay in the car and they'll come to the window.

Fries cut with the skin on

Regular or sweet potato, out of the fryer into paper — the baskets come out big enough that two people order one.

Cagney sauce runs the menu

It's on every starred burger unless you say otherwise, it's what "Cagney's Way" adds to your fries, and there's a Cagney's Cooler in the fountain.

Look for the U-Haul

It sits in front of the U-Haul lot on Meridian East, near 144th. Miss the turn once and you'll never miss it again.

What comes out the window

Handed over the sill, in paper

Meridian East, near 144th

One window, one lane, no walking up

Out & About Burgers works out of a white catering wagon on a gravel lot in South Hill — not a building, not a franchise pad, a trailer with wheels still under it.

The whole operation is bolted to the side of it. A neon OPEN in the sliding window, an ORDER HERE arrow pointing at the sill, three printed panels with the menu, and a hand-painted oval — a red marker script inside a gold car outline — big enough to read from the road.

Pull in off Meridian, roll down the window, and eat wherever you parked. That's the entire format, and 2,193 people have rated it.

How it goes

Pull in, order, go

  1. Take the lane, not a parking spot

    The drive-thru lane runs down the side of the wagon, marked by the red posts.

  2. Order at the window

    The board is right there under the sill, so you can read it while you wait your turn.

  3. Eat it in the car

    There's no dining room and there isn't meant to be. Most of the lot is somebody's table.
