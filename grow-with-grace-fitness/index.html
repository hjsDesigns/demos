Meet the trainer

It's Gracie, every session

Grow with Grace is one trainer in Enumclaw — so the person who writes your plan is the person standing next to you while you do it.

Her one-to-one sessions start with your goals, then a plan and the basics of exercise and proper form. If you prefer to train independently, her workout library gives you a separate way to get started.

The mat Pilates classes run in a small group, slow and controlled, on Cole Street. Sign-ups and the current schedule go out on her Instagram — or just text her and she'll tell you what's running.
