Meet the trainer

It's Gracie, every session

Grow with Grace Fitness is one trainer in Enumclaw. The person who writes your plan is the person standing next to you while you do it.

Her one-to-one work starts where she says it starts — goals first, then a plan, then the basics of exercise and proper form. If you would rather train independently, the workout library is a separate door into the same coaching.

The mat Pilates runs in a small group on Cole Street, slow and controlled. Sign-ups and times go out on her Instagram, or she'll just tell you if you text.
