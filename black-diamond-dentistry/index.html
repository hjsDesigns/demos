Your dentists

Two chairs, two very different roads here.

One grew up on the peninsula. One came to dentistry through a free clinic in California. They ended up in the same small office in an old coal town.

Dr. Mike was born in San Diego and grew up on the peninsula in Bremerton — soccer, wrestling and football as a kid, and biking, hiking and swimming still. He's the name patients type into Google reviews more than any other single thing about this office.

Dr. Ashley was born in Arizona and raised in California. She studied psychology at UC San Diego with a chemistry minor, started volunteering at a free dental clinic, and didn't leave dentistry after that — through the Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco, class of 2008, and up to Washington about eleven years ago. She's also the one who does facial neuromodulator injections for TMJ and clenching, which is jaw therapy first and cosmetic second.

New patient?

Three steps, then you're in the chair.

  1. Tell them what's going on

    A cleaning that's gotten away from you, a tooth that's been aching, or a question about what something costs — all fine.

  2. The front desk takes it from there

    They'll come back to you with a time. Reviews single the front desk out by name, so you'll know who you're talking to.

  3. Meet Dr. Mike or Dr. Ashley

    Whichever chair you land in, it's one of the two of them — not a rotating locum.
