Coaches

Two coaches. Every class.

Both coaches are on the mats with every class — the kids at 4:15, the adults at 6:15. Patient with a nervous first-timer, sharp with a purple belt.

  • John Proud — owner and head coach. Black belt under Jeff Hougland, December 2021. Almost two decades on the mats.
  • Shalise Ward — co-owner and coach. Purple belt, January 2024. A gymnast since childhood; teaches kids, adults and women.
  • Affiliated with Combat Sport & Fitness in Enumclaw.

Why the chess pieces

Jiu-jitsu is physical chess

Continuously solving puzzles of positions and techniques. Three things every class comes back to.

Movement

Efficient movement first. The technique follows the hips.

Breathing

Slow down, breathe, see the whole board.

Strategy

Position before submission. One square at a time.
