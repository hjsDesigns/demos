The room

A brick room on Pioneer Ave.

109 W Pioneer, in the row of old storefronts in the middle of downtown Puyallup.

The dining room is the original brick — tall arched windows onto the street, blue walls, black pendant lights hanging over long wood tables, dark wood floors. The counter runs along the back with the specials board above it and the shake flavors on a board leaning against the front of it.

Breakfast is the heart of it: eggs any style, benedicts, burritos, French toast and biscuits with sausage gravy. Burgers, sandwiches and salads run alongside all day, and the brunch glass — Bloody Marys with bacon on top, mimosas, sangria — comes out most mornings. There are a few tables on the sidewalk out front when the weather holds, and take-out and delivery both go out the door.
