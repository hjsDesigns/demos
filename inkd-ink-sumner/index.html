What she does

Two crafts, one chair

A LUX permanent makeup & tattoo suite, one block off Main Street.

"I specialize in Glitter Realism & Sticker Realism Tattoos and also a Master Permanent Makeup Artist." Staci Shantel, owner & artist

  • Glitter RealismHer own term for it — pieces built so the glitter reads as real sparkle in the skin.
  • Sticker RealismFlat colour and a white keyline, so the piece looks like a sticker laid on the arm.
  • Fine line & botanicalSingle-needle florals, small animals, locket work.
  • Script & handwritingNames and memorial pieces, including traced handwriting.

  • NR Airy Powder BrowsThe natural-results powder brow — shown healed on her own page after 1+ years.
