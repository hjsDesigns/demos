It's a bar. It's also a venue.

Behind the building there's a covered stage, a grass beer garden and string lights over the tables — the listing calls it “free pool, horseshoes, and a huge outdoor covered stage for live music.”

Before you walk in

Wood posts, red stools, and a lot of neon

Who's in there

A red building, a gravel lot, and whoever pulls in

A Harley crowd and an afternoon-burger crowd both call this place theirs, and mostly on the same day.

The saloon is a low, barn-red, shingled building set back off the Renton–Maple Valley highway with the firs right behind it. The wall by the front door is signed Parking for motorcycles only, the lot is gravel and wide open, and on a clear afternoon the bikes go in nose-first the whole length of the building.

Inside is dark panelling, wooden posts holding up the ceiling, red-topped stools and a lot of beer neon. Out the back door it opens up: grass, picnic tables, a fire pit and the covered stage. A regular writing about the place in 2025 also noted that the bar “does a lot of charity work for needy families and dogs.”

Getting there

Off the highway, past the firs

  1. Just walk in

    No reservations and no dress code. Take a stool at the bar, a table under the neon, or the grass out back when it's dry.

  2. Bring the bike

    The front wall is signed for motorcycle parking and the lot is gravel, flat and wide. Cars go around the side.

  3. Call about a group or a booking

    Bringing a crowd, or in a band with an eye on that stage? Ring the bar and ask for whoever's on.
