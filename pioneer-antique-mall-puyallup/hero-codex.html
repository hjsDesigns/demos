What's inside

Booth after booth, and then a staircase.

A vendor mall: booths on two floors, glass cases down the aisles, a comics case collectors come in for, and a wall of old Puyallup signs hung over the top of it all.

Their own card on the door: “All sales final.” Square, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover are all on the glass.

The floor

Some of what was on the floor.

Stock turns over and booths change hands, so take this as a sample rather than a catalogue.

The wall

Puyallup is hanging on the wall.

Above the cases, most of what's hanging is this town — fair boards, prize ribbons, a centennial sign. Tap a name and the wall moves in on it.

    Hunting something specific, or bringing something in to sell? Ask — that's what the counter is for.
