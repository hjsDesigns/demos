Chef Matt

Eighteen years in fine dining, then home

Matt Noesen grew up in Auburn, made a list of what to do with the rest of his life, and it came down to culinary school or golf school. He picked the French Culinary Institute in Manhattan.

Eighteen years of cooking followed — New York, Aspen, a robata restaurant in Mexico, Artisanal in Bellevue, and finally executive chef of Compass, the full-service restaurant on Microsoft's Redmond campus, where he learned to feed a vegan and vegetarian crowd from every corner of the world.

In February 2020 a family friend mentioned Zola's was for sale and wanted it to go to someone who would love it. Matt came in with his parents, ate lunch, and three months later owned the place. The promise then is the promise now: the same funky room, the local art on the walls, the vegan and vegetarian menu Zola's was known for — "we'll just offer more of it" — and everything made from scratch.

"Even with new owner Matthew Noesen's small changes to the café, and his upcoming plans to revitalize the menu, Zola's is still the top spot for coffee in Auburn."

Auburn Reporter · Best of Auburn 2025 · November 8, 2025
