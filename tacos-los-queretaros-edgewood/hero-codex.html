Straight off the board

The pizza birria, and everything under it

Their own banner puts seven things in pictures: Taquitos, Queso Birrias, Pizza Birria, Mulitas, Sopes, Torta, Burrito. The Pizza Birria is the one people drive for — a quesabirria the size of a pizza, scored into eight and sent out with consommé to dip.

The rest is a numbered board, 2 through 35, with the prices written on in marker and a green sign taped up beside it for the tacos, the drinks and the desserts. Every one of those numbers is below.

Getting there

Pull in, walk up, order

  1. Turn off Meridian

    The trailer sits back on the grass with the tent beside it. Park on the lot and walk up — there's no dining room and no waiting list.

  2. Order at the window

    The board is right there on the trailer. Give them the number, then pick your meat.

  3. Eat under the tent

    Red-check tables, string lights, salsa already out. Or take it with you — everything boxes.
