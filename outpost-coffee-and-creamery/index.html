Read the window

The whole place is taped to the glass.

There's no app and no online menu — when something new is happening at the Outpost, it goes up on the window in a plastic sleeve.

A fresh card of specials goes up every month. July's read Blueberry Chai, Raspberry Cream Italian Soda, White Gummy Bear Lotus, Campfire Coffee and Cotton Candy Freeze. Taped up around it: Rachel's cake pops, a Coca-Cola flavor card, and — just visible behind the glass — the Ghirardelli sea salt caramel bottle doing a lot of the heavy lifting.

Down in the corner, taped low where a driver can actually read it: send orders of ten or more by text, with a name and a pick-up time — and if it's a morning order, send it the night before so nobody's stuck idling. Up top is the other thing we'd never say out loud: our name on the Enumclaw Junior Rodeo out at the Expo Center.
