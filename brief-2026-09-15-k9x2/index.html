07  What broke

Your Chrome extension dropped mid-run. It answered at 3:33 PM and was unreachable by 3:56. That matters more than it sounds: Chrome is the only tool that can read a Facebook About panel, and Facebook is where small-shop emails live. Twenty-three of 31 businesses came back with no email — that number would be lower with Chrome up.

The image gate cried wolf on Kissed By Chloe. It reported two broken images; both were fetched directly and are valid 400–500 KB progressive JPEGs serving 200. The gate reads image width before a half-megabyte photo finishes decoding on a below-the-fold slot. The underlying waste is real though — those files are half a megabyte for a slot 320 px wide, worth re-encoding next time that site is touched.

Bayabelle's gate result was a 60-second browser timeout, thrown while three headless Chromiums fought over the machine. Same URL returns 200 and 16 images to curl.
