Our story

Named for Momma Bonnie.

Christina King learned these rolls at her mother's side — Bonnie Jacobs, who passed down her cinnamon-roll and dinner-roll recipes while Christina was raising toddler twins. It started with Christmas mornings.

In 2019, Christina was diagnosed with brain cancer and had to relearn to walk. Her physical therapists kept pushing her back toward the thing she loved — baking. So she baked: farmers markets first, the Buckley Kitchen in 2021, Puyallup after that, and in December 2023 she and her husband Kenny opened the counter on Highway 410.

The part nobody believes: Christina can't eat sugar. Every roll in the case is one she'll never taste.
