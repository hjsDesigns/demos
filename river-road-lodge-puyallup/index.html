What's in there

One room, four things going on

The sign bolted to the front of the building lists it in three words — bar, darts, pool. It leaves out the stage.

The stage

A real corner stage with a house drum riser, and bands loud enough to fill the whole room.

Band night, on the stage

Pool & darts

Two tables on the plank floor, barrel-top seating around them, and the dart board the front sign brags about.

The back of the room

Karaoke

Weekends, with a DJ running it. Screens at both ends so you can read the words from the pool table.

Lyrics up, mid-song

The bar

Long wood counter, taps at the near end, jukebox against the wall and the game on above it.

Looking down the bar

The stage

Somebody's always plugging in

Most bars this size have a jukebox and leave it there. This one has amps, a drum kit and Super Bowl banners strung over the players' heads.

There's no printed calendar for this — bands get booked, word goes round, and the room fills. Call the bar and ask who's on this weekend.

The room after dark

Neon, worn wood, and whoever's on the stool next to you

A red tube runs the whole length of the ceiling. Underneath it: plank floor, whiskey-barrel tables, string lights over the counter and a back bar that goes blue once the sun's down.
