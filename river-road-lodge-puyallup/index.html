One building, two names

The sign changed. The bar didn't.

Half the internet still says R&R Lounge — Google, Yelp and most of the directories haven't caught up. Same red roof at the same corner of River Road, same phone number, new name on the pole.

What's in there

One room, four things going on

The sign bolted to the front of the building lists it in three words — bar, darts, pool. It leaves out the stage.

The stage

A corner stage with a house drum riser, and amps already plugged in.

See who's been on it

Pool & darts

Two tables on the plank floor, barrel-top seating around them, and the board on the wall.

Throw one yourself

Karaoke

Screens at both ends, so you can read the words from the pool table.

Which nights

The bar

Long wood counter, taps at the near end, jukebox against the wall.

The room after dark

The stage

Somebody's always plugging in

Most bars this size have a jukebox and leave it there. This one has amps, a drum kit and Super Bowl banners strung over the players' heads.

There's no printed calendar for this — bands get booked, word goes round, and the room fills. Two things, though, never move.

The room after dark

Neon, worn wood, and whoever's on the stool next to you

A red tube runs the whole length of the ceiling. Underneath it: plank floor, whiskey-barrel tables, string lights over the counter and a back bar that goes blue once the sun's down.
