How it works

No storefront. Just Nana.

She bakes to order out of her own kitchen and brings it to you — no shop to drive to, no app in the middle.

  1. 1

    Call or text her

    Free delivery to most of Pierce County on orders of $20 or more. Further out may add a charge.

Meet Nana

Somebody's good day, five years running

Nana's started in Orting in the fall of 2019, and it has been a family thing since the first batch.

When the bakery turned one she wrote it out herself: thank you for “allowing us to be part of your special occasions, or to help you settle a craving.” That is still the whole job description. A birthday cake, a dozen rolls for a Sunday, a pie because it's Tuesday.

For a while there was a trailer — a vintage double-axle she called the Rolling Pin, wired for power with a fridge inside so she could pop up in the snow and stay warm. These days the baking happens in her own kitchen and the boxes go out in the car.

And between orders she's the one asking the neighborhood for a Santa for Orting's Home for the Holidays, or pushing a food drive for the senior center. Small town, small bakery, same thing.
