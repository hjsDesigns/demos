About Milton Lodge Bar and Grill
Milton Lodge Bar and Grill serves good food at reasonable prices. Popular items include Thursday's meatloaf, Friday's fish and chips, and dishes like popcorn shrimp with mango habanero sauce, tuna melt, and mushroom burger with jalapenos. The restaurant offers a good atmosphere and service, with options like tater tots or potato Jo's (wedges) as sides. It has a casual and friendly vibe, with a sports-bar feel due to the many TV screens. The food is delicious and well-priced, making it a great spot to visit, with highly recommended items like wings, flatbreads, and burgers.
