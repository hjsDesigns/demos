Inside Suite 103

A small room, run off one wall

It is exactly the kind of place the reviews say it is — a small shop, cooked to order, clean tables, no ceremony.

Red laminate tables and black chairs on a grey plank floor, a wood-front counter at the back, and the whole menu glowing on the screens above it. The cooler hums beside the register, and orders go out in white clamshells.

Nothing about the room is trying to be a chain. That is the point of it, and it is the one thing a third-party ordering page can never show you.
