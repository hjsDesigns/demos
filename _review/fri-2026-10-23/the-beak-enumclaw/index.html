Know the room

Pull up a stool.

A long timber bar, the whiskey wall lit up behind it, high-tops down the middle and a big post holding up the roof.

The Seahawks watch from over the bottles. Out back and up front there are horseshoe pits and a covered patio for when the weather plays along.
