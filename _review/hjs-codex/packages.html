Monthly lanes are month to month, cancel anytime. Every lane comes with a plain-language text on the 1st about what happened and what’s next.

Monthly

Run my Google

Everything on the Google side of your business, handled.

  • Your Google listing kept current: hours, photos, services and a weekly post.
  • Replies to every review, in your voice.
  • A review card for the counter and a follow-up text that brings in more reviews.
  • Your website kept current: new hours, prices, photos and specials.
  • One text on the 1st with what Google counted: calls, directions, clicks.
What I’ll need from you +

Add me as a manager on your listing, without sharing a password. Send real photos and text me when details change.

No ad budget needed. Verification timing is set by Google.

Monthly + ad budget

Run my phone

Everything in Run my Google, plus local Google ads with every call counted.

  • Google search ads planned around your business, your town and what people actually type.
  • Weekly checks to keep what works and cut what doesn’t.
  • Call tracking, so the calls the ads bring are counted, not guessed.
  • Your ad spend stays on your card, in your own Google Ads account.
  • A monthly update on calls, spend and what we’ll test next.
What I’ll need from you +

We choose an ad budget together and set up your own Google Ads account. You approve every change to the budget.

Ad budget is separate from the monthly and agreed before launch.

Before you ask

A few things,
made clear.

Still wondering about something? You can ask me directly.

Text your question ↗
Where do we start?+

Text me your business name and town, or ask for a free Google listing check. We’ll look at what you have and decide what would be most useful first.

Can I get a website without a monthly package?+

Yes. A website is a one-time build. Website care, Google Listing and the Phone Package are separate monthly services. We go over the scope and the quote together before anything starts.

What do you need from me?+

Your business details, your own photos and a little about what you want customers to do. I’ll show you a demo to review before launch. For Google work, you add me as a manager; you keep your password.

Will the site work on a phone and a laptop?+

Yes. I build and check both layouts, including navigation, readable text, image framing and the main contact action.

Who owns my website and accounts?+

You do. The website, domain, Google listing and ad account remain yours. If we stop working together, what you already own stays yours.

What happens when I need a change?+

Tell me what has changed. With website care, you can text me updates such as new hours, photos or prices. We agree on the scope of larger changes before work starts.
