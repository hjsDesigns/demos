Where do we start?+

Text me your business name and town, or ask for a free Google listing check. We’ll look at what you have and decide what would be most useful first.

Can I get a website without a monthly package?+

Yes. A website is a one-time build. Website care, Google Listing and the Phone Package are separate monthly services. We go over the scope and the quote together before anything starts.

What do you need from me?+

Your business details, your own photos and a little about what you want customers to do. I’ll show you a demo to review before launch. For Google work, you add me as a manager; you keep your password.

Will the site work on a phone and a laptop?+

Yes. I build and check both layouts, including navigation, readable text, image framing and the main contact action.

Who owns my website and accounts?+

You do. The website, domain, Google listing and ad account remain yours. If we stop working together, what you already own stays yours.

What happens when I need a change?+

Tell me what has changed. With website care, you can text me updates such as new hours, photos or prices. We agree on the scope of larger changes before work starts.
