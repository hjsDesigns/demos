I worked construction for most of my life. Long days, a crew that counted on you to show up and finish what you started. That’s where I learned what good work looks like: you don’t talk about it, you do it right, and you’re there the next morning.

In August 2026 I took a chance on something of my own. HJS Designs is me building websites and handling the Google side for the businesses I drive past every day. I started it for two reasons: the freedom to build a life on my own terms, and work that puts me across the counter from real people instead of behind a screen.

I grew up around Enumclaw and Buckley and I still live here. The businesses on this plateau are the ones I want to see win. The work is reaching further across Washington as it goes, but this is home, and I’ll always show up in person first.
