Hayden's Routine

Set Sept 8, 2026. The hours you sell in, the hours you recover in, and the things that make you you.

Weekday

7:00Up. Water. 20 min outside, no phone.
7:45–9:45Colibri, two hours. Brief, pitch reps (3× out loud, scored), planning, the community.
10:00–2:00Doors. Card, the line, owner's name + when they're in. Restaurants close the block (2–4 lull).
2:00–2:30Lunch. Log every stop in Notes. Voice-memo one real pitch for grading.
2:30–4:15Restaurant doors, or drive-and-think with music between clusters.
4:30–6:00Gym. Five days a week. Two "work days" allowed — swap in grind, no guilt.
6:00–7:00Real dinner.
7:30–11:00Home grind with Claude: follow-ups, revisions, tomorrow's route.
11:00–12:00Tea + book. No screen. Daily scan: one line in Notes. Bed at midnight.

Scans

Daily11 PM, two minutes: accomplished in body, work, people? One line.
WeeklyFri 7:30 PM, 30 min: money in · doors · closes · gym count · environment. Financial / physical / mental / spiritual each green-yellow-red. Short-term fixes only.
MonthlyFirst Friday, 90 min: the deep scan. Direction, pricing, targets, what to stop doing.

Weekend

SatColibri, then 10–12 doors (retail, salons). Groceries, car. Then off.
SunOff. Outside for real — a trail or the water. Laundry, bathroom, sheets in the evening. Backpacking lives here.

Chores

DailyDishes after dinner · ten-minute tidy before bed · log the day in Notes · bed by midnight
WeeklySun laundry + bathroom + sheets · Sat groceries + car · Wed meal-prep two dinners · Fri board review: what closed and why
