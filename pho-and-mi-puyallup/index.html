Before you walk in

White walls, warm wood, and a jungle

Why it feels like this

Somebody built this on purpose

At the bottom of their printed menu, under a drawing of two hands making a heart, is the line at the top of this page.

A River Road strip mall is not where you expect a room like this, and it is the first thing people bring up — before the food — in review after review: calm, inviting, exceptionally clean. Somebody chose every piece of it, then hung real ferns in front of the painted ones.

The cooking carries the same intent. Three broths where one would do. Zucchini noodles for anyone skipping rice. Three kinds of tea and a bubble tea neon in the window. None of that is an accident, and none of it is on the sign.

Getting there

727, on the north side of the road

  1. Walk in and order at the counter

    No reservations. Order at the front, take any table, and the bowls come out to you.

  2. Or order ahead for pickup

    They run their own online ordering — same board, same prices, packed to go.

  3. Call with a question

    Big order, allergy, or you want to know what's in a bowl before you commit — the kitchen picks up.
