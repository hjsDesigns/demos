Before you walk in

White walls, warm wood, and a jungle

The room

Somebody built this on purpose

At the bottom of their printed menu, under a drawing of two hands making a heart, it says exactly one thing about the people who run it.

The rest you read off the room. White shiplap up the walls, a warm plank floor, light-wood tables and bentwood chairs, ferns hanging in baskets, a wall of painted palm and banana leaves behind a cedar planter of live plants, and a little gold neon script glowing near the counter. It is a bright, quiet room that does not look like it belongs in a River Road strip mall — which is exactly why people keep saying so.

The cooking has the same intent behind it. Nine bowls of pho is not a hedge, it's a position: three broths, zucchini noodles for anyone skipping rice, a fried-tofu version of nearly everything, and one bowl of wok-seared beef with an egg yolk that their menu credits to Pho Thin in Hanoi. That is not a line a place writes by accident.

Getting there

727, on the north side of the road

  1. Walk in and order at the counter

    No reservations. Order at the front, take any table, and the bowls come out to you.

  2. Or order ahead for pickup

    They run their own online ordering — same board, same prices, packed to go.

  3. Call with a question

    Big order, allergy, or you want to know what's in a bowl before you commit — the kitchen picks up.
