Who's behind the counter

A family, three shifts deep

Read the reviews of this store and almost nobody talks about the shelves. They talk about the people at the register.

A mom who jokes with customers and makes them feel welcome. A dad who is helpful and gets you moving. Their son, who regulars know too. Between them they cover a store that's unlocked before dawn and still lit long after dinner, seven days a week, on a highway where most of the traffic is only passing through.

That's a long day to be the friendly face on someone's commute, and it is the single thing customers come back to write about.

Getting here

Right on the highway

  1. Watch for the awning

    Ordering a sandwich to grab on your way through? Ring the counter and it can be waiting.

    There's room off the pumps, so you're not blocking a lane while you shop.
