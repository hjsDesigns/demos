What I do

The whole job, not just the pretty part.

A website is only half of it. Somebody still has to take the photographs, write the words the way you'd say them, and fix it when your hours change.

Everything included

Here's the whole list.

All of it is included. I don't itemise a website into add-ons and I don't charge separately for showing up with a camera. If your job needs something that isn't on this list, ask me — the answer is usually yes.

Photography

Afterwards

The care plan. $50 a month.

Optional, and you can stop it whenever. Hours change, seasons change, you get a new sign or a new phone number. You text me, I fix it — usually the same day. You never open a dashboard and there's nothing for you to learn.

Hosting handled

Kept current
