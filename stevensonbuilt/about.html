Who I am

I'm Hayden. I build websites one at a time.

I'm from Buckley, Washington. Twenty-two sites so far, all of them built by me, and the number at the bottom of this page is my actual phone.

candid photo
images/hayden.jpg

I grew up in Buckley. It's a small town on the plateau southeast of Seattle, close enough to Mount Rainier that you look at the mountain out of habit on the drive to work. Enumclaw, Bonney Lake, Black Diamond, Carbonado. Those are my towns too.

Around here, the best places are the ones somebody has to tell you about. A hand-painted sign. A wall of photographs behind the register. A guy who's been running it since before I was born. You never would have found it on your own. Somebody had to bring you the first time.

Nobody knows how a place feels until they walk in. That's the problem I keep thinking about. If a place has a real website, somebody can get that feeling before they ever get in the car — the room, the people, what you're actually like in there. That's the part of this I like. Not putting a business online. Getting the feeling of the place out where people can find it.

This is what I want to do with my life.

I'm saying it plainly because it's true. This isn't a side thing and it isn't something I'm trying out until something steadier comes along. I'm betting on it working. So I take a small job more seriously than a big shop would, because it isn't one of a hundred jobs to me. It's mine, and yours is the one I'm working on.

The last thing worth telling you is that I love big mountains. Patagonia, the Alps, the Tetons. You'll see that all over my work, and it isn't a branding decision. It's just what I like looking at, and I grew up under one of them.

The trade-off

It's me. That's the whole company.

You get me every time

No account manager, no ticket queue, nobody in another time zone who has never seen your town. The person who picks your photos is the person who writes the code.

Nothing gets handed off

Your site doesn't get passed to a junior guy once the contract is signed, because there's nobody to pass it to.

Nobody's making me hit a number

If a site isn't right yet, I keep working on it. That call is mine to make and I don't have to ask anyone.

One guy has limits

There's one of me and I build one site at a time. So I'll tell you on the phone how soon I can actually start, instead of promising a date to win the job. And if what you need is bigger than one person should take on, I'll say that too.

That's the trade. You get somebody with his own name on it, and you get one person's hours in a day. I think that's a fair deal, and I'd rather you know it going in.
