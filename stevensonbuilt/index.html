Who's building it

One guy. That's the whole company.

I'm Hayden. I'm from Buckley, and I've built twenty-two of these so far — tattoo shops, a taproom, an espresso stand, a tire shop, boutiques, a jiu-jitsu gym, a couple of small-town saloons older than the pavement outside them.

No account manager, no ticket queue, nobody in another time zone who has never seen your town. The person who picks your photos is the person who writes the code is the person who picks up in two years when your hours change.
