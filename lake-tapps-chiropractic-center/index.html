The doctors

A plateau kid who never left

Dan Anderson graduated from Enumclaw High School in 1982, came back with a Doctorate of Chiropractic from Western States in 1988, and has been adjusting his neighbors ever since.

Three decades on the same stretch of Hwy 410 means a lot of his patients have been coming since before their kids could drive. His own bio puts the whole practice in one sentence: the positive energy and charisma his patients look to him for, right alongside the treatment.

Dr. Jordan Durrance joined him as the practice's second doctor. He played baseball at Pepperdine, and found chiropractic the hard way — a severe low-back injury on a high-school baseball trip to Australia. He graduated Cum Laude from Life Chiropractic College West. Outside the office he's outnumbered at home by his wife Nicole and their three kids, Jamison, Colton and Vaylin.
