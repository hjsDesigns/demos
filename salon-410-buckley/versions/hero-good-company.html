Yanka & Amanda

Two stylists, one room, since 2013

Salon 410 opened its doors in June 2013, a few steps off Highway 410 in Buckley.

Yanka and Amanda are the two names on the awning. In 2018 Courier-Herald readers voted the salon Best of the Plateau — first place — and the thank-you ad still carries both of their signatures.

It's the kind of place where the stylist asks what you want and then does exactly that. The reviews on Google and Yelp keep landing on the same word: she listened.
