Who's behind the bar now

A golf company bought a bar

Mulligans is an indoor golf club out of Buckley. In 2025 it took over this room on Main Street, and the sign on the door caught up in 2026.

There has been a bar in this building since 1974 — most of that under one family. The Buckley club has two simulator bays you can book by the hour; the plan Mulligans publishes for Sumner is one bay, in here.

Their line for the whole idea is “Swing, Sip, and Socialize in Style.” On the ground in Sumner that currently means the thing this room has always been — a full bar, screens on, pull tabs at the counter, a kitchen out back — with a bay on the way.
