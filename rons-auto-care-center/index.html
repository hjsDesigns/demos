The shop

One guy, three jobs

Ron runs the place himself — and that's the whole pitch.

There's no service writer at Ron's, no queue of technicians your car gets handed down. The person who diagnoses it is the person who fixes it and the person who explains the bill, which is why so many of the reviews are about being told the truth rather than about the repair itself.

Alongside the repair work he keeps two things running that most shops in town don't: an RV dump station that never closes, and propane filling at the same driveway. Travelers coming off Highway 410 can empty the tanks, fill the bottle and get the truck looked at without moving the rig twice.
