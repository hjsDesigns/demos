The crew

Locally owned. Eighteen years in the hut.

A small drive-thru on Witte Road that has out-poured the big green one down the street since before most of Maple Valley's kids were born.

It's one hut, one window, and a crew the regulars know by name — the kind of place where the barista remembers your order before you reach the speaker. The beans are Kerriston Coffee, the burritos are Jen's, and the pastries show up fresh every morning.

Five in the morning on weekdays, six on the weekend, seven days a week. Come join the Village.
