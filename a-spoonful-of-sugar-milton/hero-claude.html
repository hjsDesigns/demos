Four ways in

Walk up, drive through, or order it ahead

It's a small house with a counter, a handful of farmhouse tables and a window around the back. Whichever way you come at it, the same kitchen is making it.

Doors at five

Mon–Fri · earliest in town

Weekday mornings the lights come on at 5 AM, which is well before almost anything else on Milton Way.

Drive-thru around back

Same menu, no parking

The window is at the rear of the building. Parking back there is tight when the line builds — that's the one thing regulars warn about.

Before you walk in

White chairs, oak tables, and a wall of Mary Poppins

Across the top of the board

Five they made up themselves

Above every price on the wall there's a row of five house drinks with no prices next to them — just names. Tap a cup and it fills with what's actually in it.

Tap a cup.

Any of them, hot or iced, in any of the four cup sizes.

Why everything has that name

The whole place is a Mary Poppins joke

Start with the name. Then read the board: Supercali Club, Practically Perfect Gobbler, Jane's Classic BLT, Michael Porker, Uncle Albert's Scramble, Element of Fun Salad. It goes all the way down.

There's a framed film poster on the wall, a shelf of teacups and snow globes under it, and a room full of white spindle-back chairs and honey-oak tables that look like somebody's grandmother's kitchen — because that's the point. People come for a $5 breakfast sandwich and stay because the room is comfortable.

The coffee is Dillanos, roasted up the road in Sumner, and it goes into everything from a $2 drip to the layered lotus drinks people photograph on the counter. They've been at it long enough to run a promo on their own cups reading “14% off today, in honor of 14 years.”

Getting here

2420 Milton Way, the white one

  1. Come in and read the wall

    The board is the whole menu, and it takes a minute — there's no reservation, no wait list, just the counter.

  2. Or take the drive-thru

    Around the back of the building. Go slow through the lot, it's tight when there's a line.

  3. Ask what this month's special is

    There's a specials board that changes with the calendar. It's the thing regulars order.
