The shop

A counter, and a dining room

Yoko Teriyaki is the corner suite at 2800 Milton Way — a teriyaki grill and a Chinese wok working side by side behind one counter.

You order up front and eat in, or call ahead and carry it out. The dining room is small and quiet: white tables, black chairs, and cherry-blossom screens across the front windows.

Grilled teriyaki plates, Chinese wok dishes, bowls, ramen and udon, gyoza and hand-battered tempura, and a short list of rolls. Their own teriyaki sauce, spicy sauce and salad dressing go home by the bottle.
