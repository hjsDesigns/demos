The story

The chef it’s named for

Sumalee’s is exactly what the sign says — one chef’s small, family-owned kitchen. Chef Sumalee grew up cooking in Southern Thailand, and you can taste it in the curries.

The room is warm and easy — salmon walls, a slate fireplace, art on every side — and the kitchen happily cooks around allergies.
