Josh & Erica Smart

They bought the bookstore because they missed one

Before it was The Dusty Shelf, this address was The Sequel. Josh and Erica Smart bought it in the spring of 2023 and reopened that May.

“We, on vacation, like to visit used book stores.”

— Josh Smart

What they came home wanting was the kind of shop you plan a detour around — shelves deep enough to get lost in, and a counter that keeps you there. So the coffee came in, and then the wine, and then the tables filled up with people who had only meant to stop for a minute.

The rest of it grew out of the room. Book clubs on the third week, story time on Wednesday mornings, local authors at the Chamber's wine walk, and a space that Enumclaw groups use during the day — Erica's plan from the start was to find different community events to use this space, and that's more or less what happened.
