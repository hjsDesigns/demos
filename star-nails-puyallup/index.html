Whose chair it is

Everyone in Puyallup just says “ask for Chita”

Read the Google reviews and one name comes back twenty-six times in the first forty-five. Customers spell it Chita, Chi, once even Cheetah. It's one woman, and this is her shop.

She works on her own. “She said that she works by herself, and she's been doing nails for 20 years,” one customer wrote two years ago. Another calls the place “a one woman show.” A third likes “the privacy of being the only customer.” One older review simply names her “Chita (owner).”

So the reference picture on your phone goes straight to the person holding the brush — no front desk, no hand-off. “I showed her a reference picture and she matched it perfectly.”
