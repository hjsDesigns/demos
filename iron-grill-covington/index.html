How a Mongolian grill works

You build it. They grill it.

No menu to decode and nothing to order off a list. You walk the line with an empty bowl, stack whatever you want in it, hand it over, and it comes back as your plate.

1

Grab a bowl

Three sizes are stacked where the line starts, each with its own price and its own little card. Take the one you’re hungry for — child bowls are marked 10 years and under.

2

Pile it high

Vegetables sliced by hand, noodles, rice, meat and seafood, then the sauces at the end. Fill it as high as you can stack it — one trip, one price.

3

Watch it hit the iron

Your bowl goes straight onto the big round grill in front of you and comes back on a black plate. Rice, noodles or both — the board says that part is up to you.
