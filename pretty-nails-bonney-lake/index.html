The shop

The same hands, year after year

“I appreciate your time leaving a very kind feedback for our store. We are happy to make our customers happy. See you soon.”

That is how the shop signs off on nearly every review it answers, and it has signed off that way for years. The names underneath barely change either — Jenny (Jennifer, to the people who have been coming longest), Sue, LT and Le Le, at the same stations since at least 2021, with Jennifer's husband on the pedicure massage.

The room got its refresh a few years back and it is unusually committed to the idea: terracotta walls, three arched doorways with chandeliers behind them, plaster columns down the middle of the floor, and a lit aquarium between the two halves of the room.
