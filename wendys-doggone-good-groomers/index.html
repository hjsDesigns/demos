The shop

Rubber-topped table, lavender wall, a row of crates

It's a small shop, and somebody's hands are on your dog the whole time it's in there.

The appointment card has a line printed on it that reads “your groomer is:” — you get handed a name, not a ticket number. The reviews bear that out: people come back asking for the groomer they had last time.

    The breed, the kind of coat, and roughly how long it's been since the last one.

    No app, no account. One phone call and the dog's on the book.
