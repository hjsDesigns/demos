The stand

A little cedar building in the Masonic lot.

Board-and-batten cedar, a metal roof, string lights strung the length of it, and the carved oval sign up on the gable.

It sits off 264th Ave SE in the Enumclaw Masonic Center lot, on the way out of town toward the mountain. Pull in on the gravel, roll your window down, and the whole thing happens through theirs — no parking, no line inside, no counter.

A paper punch card on the counter. Seasonal drinks on a hand-lettered chalkboard. The lights come on at four in the morning on weekdays, which is earlier than anywhere else in town.
