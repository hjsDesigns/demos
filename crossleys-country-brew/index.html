The stand

A little cedar building in the Masonic lot.

Board-and-batten cedar, a metal roof, string lights strung over the service window, and a carved oval sign up on the gable.

It sits off 264th Ave SE in the Enumclaw Masonic Center lot, on the way out of town toward the mountain. Pull in on the gravel, roll your window down, and the whole transaction happens through theirs.

A paper punch card on the counter. Seasonal drinks on a hand-lettered chalkboard. The little details are part of the stop.
