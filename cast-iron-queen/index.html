The yard

Eat it outside

The truck sits off 268th with the fence line on one side and a field of sunflowers on the other. You order at the window and eat it at the tables.

  • Outdoor seating in the yard — no dining room, no waiting list.
  • Parking on site, and it is wheelchair accessible.
  • Takeaway if you would rather eat it at home — cards accepted at the window.

Meet the Queen

Melissa runs the window

Made from scratch, sauced by hand, handed to you in a gingham-lined box.

The Monster Cinnamon Roll is a six-inch, made-from-scratch roll smothered in cream cheese frosting — “it’s as big as your head,” her menu says. “Enough for four people!”

Every dipping sauce is 100% homemade, and the Nashville hot is her own sweet-‘n-spicy recipe. The chicken strip basket comes with one sauce, and, as she puts it, “you’ll want to get extra!!”
