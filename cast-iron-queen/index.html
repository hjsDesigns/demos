The yard

Eat it on the grass

She parks on the lawn off 268th — black picnic tables under red umbrellas, a mown field on one side and the foothills straight ahead. You order at the window and eat it out there.

  • Picnic tables under umbrellas — no dining room, no waiting list.
  • Lawn games out on the grass while you wait.
  • Parking on site, and it is wheelchair accessible.
  • Takeaway if you would rather eat it at home — cards at the window.

Meet the Queen

Melissa runs the window

Made from scratch, sauced by hand, handed to you in a gingham-lined box.

There is no building. The whole kitchen is one polished vintage Airstream with a service window cut into its flank, towed in behind a teal Ford pickup that carries her crown-and-skillet badge on the door.

Six of the seven lunch plates on her board are fried chicken; the seventh is smoked brisket. The sauces are hers too — the Queen Sauce, the Loaded Fry sauce, the sweet-‘n-spicy Nashville hot.
