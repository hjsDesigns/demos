The part that changes

New boards go up all year

Two boards run alongside the painted one: a printed Sweet Specials sheet taped in the window, and a chalkboard out front that gets rewritten by hand. Here's a strawberry-season run of each — worth asking what's on them today.

  • Strawberry Shortcake

    Strawberry flavor with shortbread, topped with cream and dried strawberries.

  • Dipped Berry

    White chocolate with fresh strawberry flavor.

  • Berry Dreamsicle

    Orange creamsicle with a berry and cream twist.

  • Strawberry Sally

    Lemonade drizzled with strawberry purée — and, the sheet promises, a special surprise.

What the lane looks like

Twenty-odd years on River Road

Est. 2003 — and still a hut

It's printed right on the cup: the Bean Tree, Est. 2003. Same small brown stucco box in the River Road Plaza lot, more than twenty years on.

Two windows, a red neon strip along the roofline and an espresso machine pulling Dillanos — the Sumner roaster whose truck backs up to the stand on delivery mornings. Everything else on the board comes out of the same twelve feet: the smoothies, the spritzers, the bagels, the fritters on the chalkboard.

The chalkboard out front is the tell. Under the donut list, in a different colour, they write the names of the baristas working that day. Regulars put the rest plainly in a review this July: “There is no better coffee stand. Fight me about it.”

Getting your coffee

Pull in off River Road

  1. Turn into the plaza

    The stand sits in the River Road Plaza lot by Tractor Supply and Dollar Tree — the little brown building with the red strip along the roof.

  2. Take the lane either side

    Two windows, so the line moves. There's a walk-up door too if you'd rather park.

  3. Ask what's on the boards

    The chalkboard and the Sweet Specials sheet both change. If there are fritters, they'll say so out front.
