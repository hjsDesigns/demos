Google Ads

Monthly · intro pricing

Ads put you at the top of the page when somebody nearby searches for the thing you sell — today, not eventually. Two separate pots of money, and they never mix:

Your ad budget

You pick it. It stays on your card.

This is the money that goes to Google to actually show the ads. It never passes through me. You are only charged when somebody actually clicks — not for showing up. We set the monthly number together and you can change it any time.

My fee

Introductory. Ask me.

Writing the ads, choosing the search terms, setting the area they run in, and checking them every week to cut what wastes money and push what works.

What it can do

  • Catch people at the exact moment they are searching — "custom cakes near me", "birthday cake Enumclaw", "wedding cake".
  • Show up for the busy stretches: holidays, graduation, wedding season.
  • Every call and message from an ad gets counted, so you know exactly what it did.

Straight talk

  • Ads need somewhere to land. Paying for clicks that arrive at a listing you do not control is money spent badly — the site comes first.
  • They take a few weeks of tuning before the spend gets efficient.
  • They stop the day you stop paying. Unlike the site, you are renting the attention.
  • The free step comes first. Claiming your Google Business Profile and getting the right phone, hours and website on it costs nothing, and I do that before anyone spends a dollar on ads.
