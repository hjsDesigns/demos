The bakery

A small kitchen at Krain Corner

In her own words: “We are a family run bakery specializing in everything homemade from scratch.”

Baked by Shalyn sits in the cream-and-green cottage where 264th meets the highway, a few minutes out of downtown Enumclaw. Cheesecakes, pies and cinnamon rolls go in the case in the morning; the cakes get built to order in the back.

Everything on the board is baked here — twenty-five cheesecakes, twenty-eight pies, eleven kinds of cinnamon roll, and whatever cake somebody described over the counter that week. Whole pies want two days' notice. Everything else, come in and look through the glass.

Ordering

Tell her what you're picturing

  1. Say what it's for

    A wedding, a sixth birthday, a Sunday. That's usually enough to start.

  2. Send the picture in your head

    People text or bring in a photo of the cake they want. She works from it.

  3. Give her the lead time

    Whole pies need at least 48 hours. Cakes, ask — a week's notice has been plenty before.
