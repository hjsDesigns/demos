The Velasco family

Thirty-five years on the Black Diamond road

Juan and Alvaro Velasco opened El Caporal in 1991 with family recipes and a dining room built to last.

The name is the ranch foreman — the caporal on horseback who rides across the printed menu. The food is what the Velascos grew up on: tacos, burritos and arroz con pollo from family recipes, chile colorado and chile verde simmered slow, fajitas for two on a hot plate, and camarones five ways. Fresh ingredients, big portions, chips before you've finished sitting down.

Maple Valley has grown up around the place. Regulars on the review sites count their visits in decades, not years, and the room they walk into is the one they remember — carved pine booths, terracotta walls, tin lanterns, and the bar for the game.
