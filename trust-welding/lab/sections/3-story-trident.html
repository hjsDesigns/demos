The story

The trident on the Mariners' door

Trustin runs Trust Welding out of a one-man shop in Enumclaw, next to D Watt Custom Fab, and out of the truck anywhere in King County. The badge says Est. 2025. The work says the rest: the trident cut for the Mariners' secret cocktail door, a Corten fire pit cladded in Snohomish, track bars for a Duramax, a motor mount for a Cat rebuild.
