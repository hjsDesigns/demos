3 · His exact logo

Instagram profile badge, 150 × 150

That is the only size Instagram serves and the only place it exists online (no Facebook, no website). The 4× enlargement is soft. The vector or the original file has to come from Trustin or whoever drew it. Colours in it: black, white, steel grey, and the spark blue (about #24B7CA).

Nano Banana Pro with the badge as the reference, one shot each, 12 credits. The badge's layout survived in all six. "Just welded" and "Plate hot" are the two closest to your words; "Chrome on black" is the most respectable; "Corten" is the Snohomish fire pit.

6 · Six contact pages for Trustin (Higgsfield renders)

Rendered by Higgsfield from his chrome emblem, one shot each, 12 credits. Every one carries "Tell me about the job", the four fields, "Send the request", and Text 425-283-6496 · Call · Instagram. Say a number and it gets built for real (desktop and phone).

1 · Work orderA shop ticket clipped to the bench under the light: the form reads like paperwork a welder already fills out.
2 · Bolted plateThe form on black powder-coated steel, bolted to concrete under one hanging shop light. The most "his shop" of the six.
3 · Photo firstThe job photo drop is the biggest thing on the page, because a photo is what gets a real quote back.
4 · Stencil signGunmetal panel, one hazard-blue stripe, stencil letters. Reads like an industrial sign, not a web form.
5 · Welded frameA light-grey panel held in a welded steel frame, beads showing at the corners. Classiest of the six.
6 · Truck doorHis badge on the door of a black work truck with the rig in the bed, the form on a dark plate below. The mobile-welding story in one frame.
