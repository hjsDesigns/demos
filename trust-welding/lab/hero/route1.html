Straight answer: no stock photo of Enumclaw town with Rainier behind it exists. iStock has 30 "Enumclaw" frames and zero aerials; Alamy has none at all. The geometry explains it: Rainier sits south-south-east of downtown and Mt Peak, the only hill, is also south-east, so from its top the town is behind you when you face the mountain. The frame you want is a drone shot from the fields north of town, or nothing. Nobody has put one on a stock site.
