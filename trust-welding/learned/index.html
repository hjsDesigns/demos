What I do

Welding and metal fabrication, in the shop or at your site

Custom pieces built to the job, and mobile welding when the job can’t come to the shop. Steel, aluminum and Corten.

  • Custom fabrication: signs, gates, railings, planters, racks, counters, etc.
  • Mobile welding across King County
  • Structural and equipment repair
  • Truck and trailer work: track bars, headache racks, roof racks, D-rings, and more

Trucks and trailers

Duramax track bars to a Cat motor mount

Track bars for a Duramax, aluminum roof-rack bars, a headache rack, D-rings on a roll-off bed. Measured on the truck, welded, back on the road.

Steel for outside

Corten in Snohomish. Planters that stay out.

A Corten fire pit in Snohomish, custom steel planter boxes, a sign for Hopper Group. Corten is built for weather: it rusts to a skin and stops.

The barista bar rail at Colibri Coffee Roasters, the trident cut-out for the Mariners’ cocktail door, the install at Premier Chiropractic. Built in the shop, set in place on site.
