Meet the groomer

Sarah Kurtti is the whole shop.

One groomer, one van, four towns on the plateau.

There is no front desk and no second chair. Sarah is the person who answers the phone, pulls up outside, does the bath, and hands the dog back — so the same hands know your dog every visit.

She works Tuesday through Saturday, with the long days early in the week, and books one household at a time through her own request form. Right now her list is full enough that she is only taking returning clients back.
