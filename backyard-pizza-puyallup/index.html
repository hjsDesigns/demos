Pull into the gravel

It really is a backyard

There's no dining room. There's a gravel yard off South Meridian, a trailer painted end to end, and a window you walk up to.

The sign at the road says PIND KOHALA. The trailer with the skateboarding burger and pizza slice glowing on its sign is Backyard. Order at that window, then take a picnic table under the lights that run the length of the yard, or carry the box home.

Two things are taped inside the glass, and they tell you most of what the place is: the Tacoma-Pierce County food-safety placard, rated GREAT, and the house notice — no smoking, no alcoholic beverages, no drugs on the premises.
