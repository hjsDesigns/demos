The restaurant

One kitchen that does all of it.

A sit-down teriyaki house with a sushi bar, in the plaza on 214th Ave E.

Most teriyaki places pick a lane. This one runs a grill, a wok and a sushi bar out of the same kitchen — so a table can order a chicken teriyaki plate, a Mongolian beef and a sashimi deluxe and have it all land at once. Two hundred and one dishes, from a $2 scoop of rice to a $39 sashimi special.
