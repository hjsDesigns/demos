Inside Unit L

Your table in Bonney Lake.

A sit-down teriyaki house with its own sushi bar, in the plaza on 214th Ave E.

Pick from the grill, the wok or the sushi bar. A chicken teriyaki plate, Mongolian beef, a sashimi deluxe — there’s room for everyone’s favorite at the table.

Lit menu boards fill the wall over the counter, the tables are solid walnut, and there's counter seating down the far side under the sushi lanterns. The online menu lists 201 dishes; the photographed in-house menu adds tartare, tataki and carpaccio.
