Some places fit into your day.
This one can change with it.

Vintage brings the coffee shop and the wine bar together in one room: tall windows, wood shelves lined with bottles, and a counter that takes you from espresso to a glass of wine.

There’s a drive-through for the mornings that move quickly, and tables inside for the ones that don’t. Come back for a board to share, a live set, or a creative evening with your neighbors.
