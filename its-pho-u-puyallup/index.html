Why it's called that

The name is the joke, and they commit

The phone number spells it out: 253-848-PHOU.

Open the menu and there's a cartoon on the cover — someone down on one knee holding out a steaming bowl, hearts in the air, a speech bubble reading “It's Pho U!!” — printed under a red-and-green striped awning. The same two characters are stuck to the window by the front door. That's the whole personality of the place, and they put it on everything they hand you.

Behind it is a bright room off South Meridian: light plank floors, a cedar wall with plants growing out of it, dark wood chairs, round glass lights over the tables. Phở, char-broiled rice plates and yakisoba all under fifteen dollars, vegan and vegetarian options through the menu, take-out and delivery if you'd rather eat at home.
